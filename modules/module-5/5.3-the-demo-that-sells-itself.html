5.3 The Demo That Sells Itself

Showing a live, working Base44 app on the call instead of pitching an idea.

Before you start this lesson

  • A niche template you can duplicate and customise live
  • A booked conversation with a real prospect

The single biggest edge you have over a traditional agency pitch is that you can show a working product, not a mockup or a slide. This lesson is about using that edge on the actual call.

Base44 step Before the call, duplicate your 3.2 template and quickly rebrand it with the prospect's actual business name and a plausible version of their data. This takes 10-15 minutes and changes the entire conversation from "here's an idea" to "here's yours, already built."

Call structure

  1. 2 minutes: ask about how they currently handle [the specific problem you identified] — let them describe the pain in their own words.
  2. 5 minutes: share your screen and walk through the rebranded demo, tying each feature directly back to what they just described.
  3. 3 minutes: price and next steps (see 5.4 for handling pushback here).

Copy this

Pre-call prep prompt paste into any AI chat 
I have a demo call with [BUSINESS NAME], a [NICHE] business. What I know about their current situation: [WHAT YOU LEARNED FROM RESEARCH/OUTREACH].

Give me 3 specific open-ended questions to ask at the start of the call to get them talking about their current pain points in their own words, so I can tie my demo directly back to what they say.
What you should have when this lesson is done A rebranded demo app ready to show, plus your 3 opening questions written out before the call.
