Module 5: Client Acquisition Lesson 1 of 5

5.1 Who to Contact and How to Find Them

Turning your Module 2 lead list into a prioritized outreach order.

Before you start this lesson

  • Your lead list from 2.2, filtered to your locked-in niche
  • Your client pipeline app from 3.3, ready to log contacts

You already have real leads sitting in your Lead Finder app from 2.2. This lesson is about deciding who to contact first, not finding new leads.

Prioritization framework

Rank your leads using the signals from 2.3:

  • Tier 1: no website, or a clearly outdated one, plus a working phone number. Highest signal of both need and reachability.
  • Tier 2: has a basic website but no online booking/portal, working contact info.
  • Tier 3: everything else — worth keeping in the list, not worth leading with.

Copy this

Prioritization prompt paste into any AI chat 
I have a list of local business leads in [NICHE/AREA] with data on whether they have a website, online booking, and working contact info.

Help me write a simple scoring rule I can apply to each lead by hand in my Base44 Lead Finder app (no website + working phone = highest priority, etc.) so I contact the highest-signal businesses first instead of just going in list order.
What you should have when this lesson is done Your top 20 leads marked/sorted by priority in your Lead Finder app, ready for the scripts in 5.2.
