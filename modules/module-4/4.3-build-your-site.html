4.3 Build Your Site in Base44

The proper version of your Day 2 page — and itself a demonstration of what you sell.

Before you start this lesson

  • Your positioning sentence from 4.1
  • Your name, colours and font from 4.2
  • Your Day 2 site from Module 0

Your site has an unusual job. It isn't really a marketing page — at your stage almost nobody discovers you through search. It's the thing a prospect checks after you have contacted them, deciding whether you're real.

That makes it a trust document, and it means it should be short.

What goes on it

  • Your positioning sentence, large, at the top. The 4.1 sentence, unchanged.
  • What you build — three concrete examples with screenshots of things you have actually made. Module 0 gave you five.
  • How it works — three steps, an honest timeline. People want to know what they're agreeing to before they reply.
  • Who you're — two sentences and a photo. A local business is hiring a person.
  • A contact form. You have built several this week.

What stays off: stock photos of people in meetings, a blog you won't write, testimonials you don't have, and any claim you can't back.

Site build prompt paste into Base44, fill in your details 
Rebuild my one-page business site properly.

My positioning: [YOUR 4.1 SENTENCE]
Business name: [NAME]
Colours: [TWO COLOURS]

Sections in this order:
1. Big headline with my positioning sentence and a contact button
2. "What I build" — three cards with room for a screenshot, title and two lines each
3. "How it works" — three numbered steps: free consultation, I build it in about a week, you own it
4. "About" — short bio with space for a photo
5. Contact form saving to a list I can see

Clean, confident, fast-loading. No stock imagery.

The part most people miss

Say on the site that it was built on the same platform you build client work on. It sounds like a small detail and it does real work: your site is the demo. A prospect looking at a fast, clean page you made is already most of the way to believing you can make one for them.

What you should have when this lesson is done A published site on your own domain with your real positioning, at least three screenshots of things you built in Module 0, and a working contact form.
