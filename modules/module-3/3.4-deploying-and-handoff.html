3.4 Deploying & Handing Off a Build

The exact mechanics of publishing and handing an app to a client — the part that's easy to fumble the first time.

Before you start this lesson

  • A finished build from 3.2 worth deploying
  • Your client pipeline app from 3.3

Building something great and then fumbling the handoff is a real way to lose a client's confidence right at the finish line. This lesson is a checklist you'll reuse for every real delivery starting in Module 7.

The handoff checklist

  1. Publish the app in Base44 and confirm the live URL loads correctly in a private/incognito window (not just your logged-in editor view).
  2. Custom domain (optional but strong): if the client has a domain, connecting it (e.g. app.theirbusiness.com) makes this feel like their software, not a demo you built.
  3. Access: decide who logs in and how — the client themselves, their staff, or just you managing it for them. Set this deliberately, don't leave it as an afterthought.
  4. Data export: confirm the client can get their own data out if they ever needed to (even if they never will) — this is a trust signal that costs you nothing to offer.
  5. A short walkthrough: record or do live a 5-minute "here's how you use this" so they're not guessing on day one.
