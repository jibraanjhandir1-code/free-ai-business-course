0.3 Day 3: A Tool You Will Actually Use

Build something for yourself, so you learn data models on a project where mistakes cost nothing.

Before you start this lesson

  • 20–25 minutes
  • Something in your life you currently track badly

Every client build you ever ship comes down to the same thing: records, relationships between them, and a view that makes them useful. Today you learn that on your own data, where getting it wrong costs nothing.

Pick something you genuinely track badly right now. Job applications, expenses, a reading list, gym sessions, things you have lent people. It doesn't need to be business-related and it's better if you actually want it.

Today's build~25 min

A personal tracker with at least two connected pieces of data. The connection is the part that matters — one record linked to another, not just a flat list.

Adapt the prompt below to whatever you picked.

Day 3 build prompt paste into Base44, swap in your own thing 
Build a personal tracker app for [WHAT YOU WANT TO TRACK].

I want two connected types of record:
- [MAIN THING] with fields for [FIELDS]
- [RELATED THING] that belongs to a [MAIN THING], with fields for [FIELDS]

Give me a dashboard showing totals and the most recent entries, and let me add and edit records easily.

Example if I were tracking job applications: Companies, and Applications that belong to a Company.

Then put real data in it

Add at least five real entries. Not test data — actual things from your life. Two reasons: you find out immediately whether the structure works, and you end the day with a tool you'll open again, which is worth more than a perfect empty app.

What you should have when this lesson is done A published tracker with two connected record types and at least five real entries in it.
