Module 7: 1-Week Sprint Blueprint Lesson 2 of 4

7.2 Days 1–3: Building the Deliverable in Base44

The real build — combining everything from Module 3 into your first client's actual product.

Before you start this lesson

  • A signed scope doc or clear verbal agreement from 5.5 on exactly what's being built
  • Your 3.2 "Template — Client Build" project, duplicated fresh for this client
  • The client's actual business details (name, branding, real workflow specifics) — not placeholders

This is where the template from 3.2 pays off. You aren't starting from a blank screen — you're customizing something that already works.

Base44 step Duplicate your Template — Client Build project, rename it for this client, and prompt Base44 with the specifics from their scope doc.

The prompt

Base44 build prompt adapt to your client's scope, paste into your duplicated template 
This app is being customized for [CLIENT BUSINESS NAME], a [NICHE] business.

Based on our agreed scope:
[PASTE THE "WHAT'S INCLUDED" SECTION FROM YOUR 5.5 SCOPE DOC]

Update the app to match this exactly — rename fields, adjust the workflow/statuses to match how this business actually operates, and add their branding (business name, and colors if provided). Keep everything outside this scope as-is for now.

Day-by-day inside the build

  1. Day 1: customize structure and fields to match the scope exactly; get the core workflow right before touching polish.
  2. Day 2: add any agent/automation pieces from Module 6 if they're in scope; enter realistic sample data so the demo feels real, not empty.
  3. Day 3: full walkthrough yourself as if you were the client; fix anything that feels confusing before they ever see it. Publish.
What you should have when this lesson is done A published, client-branded app that matches the agreed scope exactly, ready to demo.
